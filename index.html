            Saving & shifts

            Everything (routes, tonight's taps, notes and report details) is saved on this phone after every tap. Closing the app or turning off the phone won't lose anything. Clearing Safari's website data would erase it, so keep a copy of your route (Copy route) in Notes.

            Save a shift to keep a copy of that shift only: its door marks, entries, door check times, checklist and report details. Restoring a shift replaces just the current shift. Your routes, officer names and freezers are never changed by it. (To back up a route, use Copy route above.)
